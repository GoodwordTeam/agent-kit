# A1 host-corpus rerun, 2026-09-28

A rerun of the A1 host corpus after PR #15, which gave scaffolds to 21 positive cases. On
2026-09-25 only 3 positives could run, and a positive with no scaffold measures the base model
rather than the skill (ADR 0002). The question for this run: with the positives scaffolded, does
the plugin arm beat the no-plugin arm, case by case?

The short answer:

- **No measurable difference.** Over 56 cases with graded runs in both arms, the plugin arm passed
  39 of 170 runs and the no-plugin arm 33 of 168. The mean paired delta is **+0.04 [−0.04, 0.11]**
  on pass rate and +0.01 [−0.04, 0.07] on the host's weighted score. Both intervals cross 0.
- **Scaffolded positives do not separate the arms either.** 18 of the 21 ran: 11/54 with the
  plugin, 10/54 without, delta +0.02 [−0.07, 0.11]. The skill loaded in 27 of 30 runs that carry
  a load indicator, so the skills are reached; the runs fail after that.
- **Most failures are the harness, not the skills.** Of 42 classified cases with a negative delta
  or 0/3 in both arms, 25 are scaffold or fixture defects, 9 grader defects, 4 skill defects and
  4 model variance. The largest single cause is a prompt that presumes earlier work in an empty
  workspace (17 cases). The second is that `git` cannot run inside the host sandbox (4 cases, and
  reported in nearly every Bash-granted run).
- **Negatives are the only group with an interval that clears 0**, and only just: +0.08
  [0.00, 0.17] on pass rate.
- **The run is partial.** The budget ran out with 8 cases unrun and one case run once. About $10
  to $12 finishes them.

Everything here is a screening figure: one subject, three runs per case and arm.

## Scaffolded positives against 2026-09-25

None of the 21 has a 2026-09-25 figure. All of them were tagged `needs-fixture` then and excluded,
so this run is their first measurement, and there is no before-and-after for them.

- **18 ran.** The other three (`babysit-pr/failing-check-is-repaired-within-the-cap`,
  `compound-refresh/drifted-path-is-updated-in-place`, `ultraqa/five-cycles-is-the-ceiling`)
  belong to skills the default install does not ship, so `eval-local.sh` never selects them. That
  is the same gap A2 closed with `ak build --profile all`.
- **The scaffolds work as setup.** In the with-plugin arm the skill loaded 27 of 30 times, and the
  replies read the planted repository, tickets and records. Scaffolding did what ADR 0002 asked:
  these positives now reach the skill.
- **They still do not separate the arms.** 11/54 against 10/54. Only
  `diagnose-emits-a-packet-where-no-grant-covers-the-fix` (+0.67) and
  `a-serious-issue-in-an-untouched-affected-caller-stays-reportable` (+0.33) favour the plugin;
  12 of the 18 are 0/3 in both arms. The classification below says why: in most of them the
  sandbox or the grants stop the skill's own steps, or the grader fails replies that appear to
  meet it.

**The 40 cases both runs share** make a rough replicate, since the only non-eval change between
`449efa8` and `6a87375` is a seven-line edit to `protocols/evidence-gate/PROTOCOL.md`. Pooled, the
plugin arm went from 35/120 to 28/116 and the no-plugin arm from 28/120 to 23/114. Five cases moved
by two or more passes in one arm, in both directions: `a-readiness-lane-vetoes-the-panel-verdict`
was 2/3 against 0/3 and is now 0/3 against 2/3. At three runs per arm, a one- or two-run swing in a
single case is noise.

What the classes point at:

- **The empty-workspace cases are the `needs-fixture` problem under another tag.** They are
  adversarial, negative and missing-tools cases whose prompts refer to plans, rounds, tickets,
  findings or drafts that the run does not contain. Every session answers the same way: it cannot
  find the thing, and it asks for it. That never reaches the behaviour the grader checks.
- **`git` does not work inside the host sandbox.** `/usr/bin/git` is the Xcode shim, and the
  sandbox blocks the cache write it needs, so it fails with `Failed to locate 'git'`. Every
  Bash-granted group reports this in nearly every run of both arms. The scaffolds build real
  repositories, but the session cannot read their history, and skills that need a worktree or a
  revision stop.
- **Some cases grant too little.** All 14 `super-review` and `receiving-review` cases that ran
  declare no gated tool, so they have no shell. A review skill that has to diff two revisions
  cannot do it there.
- **Prose prompts to user-invoked skills cannot discriminate** when the grader needs the skill's
  own vocabulary. The invocation law keeps the skill unloaded, so both arms are the base model.
- **Four skill findings stand**, each at three runs: `doc-review`'s presented form drifts from its
  four buckets, `diagnose` skips its loop-first discipline once loaded, `diagnose` does not load on
  a fix-attempt-cap prompt, and `super-align` stops on a missing knowledgebase instead of asking.
